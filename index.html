How we work

Led by Dan Bridge. One contact, three disciplines.

With 30 years’ hands-on experience, Dan works inside your organisation to understand how it runs, find where the time and money go, and build the fix. Consultants advise but don’t build. Developers build but don’t understand your business. Your IT provider keeps things running. We do the bit in between.

  • Understand. Business analysis: how work really happens.
  • Find. Data science: where time and cost are leaking, and what your records can tell you.
  • Build. Engineering: the fix, made properly.

Embedded with you, not parachuted in. Read Dan’s background ↗

Larger organisations

Legacy systems, made modern.

Some of our work is on a larger scale: moving old databases onto modern platforms without losing years of records or stopping day-to-day work. We work inside your team, starting with discovery, agreeing a plan and delivering in phases. One named person stays accountable throughout: Dan Bridge, who spent twenty years running healthcare systems across NHS Wales and the university network.

  • Discovery and a clear plan before anything is built.
  • Phased delivery, so normal work carries on.
  • Documentation and ongoing support, so you’re not dependent on us.
  • Security, data protection and accessibility built in from the start.
