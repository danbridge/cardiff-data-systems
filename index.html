01 / The approach

The work that matters is often hidden beneath the surface.

I take legacy, poorly documented systems and help turn them into something clear, dependable, and fit for the people who rely on them every day.

I’ve spent three decades as a hands-on software engineer, moving into CTO and technical leadership roles along the way, grounded in a Computer Science degree. My work combines technical depth with the practical business of getting the right thing built — including deep, hands-on experience writing complex queries, normalising relational schemas, moving legacy and monolithic applications onto cloud platforms like GCP and AWS, and designing the APIs that tie it all together.

I'm particularly drawn to organisations with a strong mission to help people. The technology matters most when it's in service of something worth doing.

02 / How I work

One person who understands the problem and the code.

I sit down directly with the small groups and individuals who actually run things — not through a layer of business analysts — to properly understand their problems and goals, then lead the technical design and development with a small, hands-on team. It keeps decisions clear, communication direct, and the work grounded in what people actually need.

I also make a point of writing things down clearly as we go, and making those documents freely available so anyone in your organisation can read them, so your understanding of your own systems doesn't end up living inside one person's head — including mine. If people move on, or priorities change, the people who stay should still be able to understand and run what's been built.

03 / Selected work

Cardiff University, NHS Wales & the Welsh University Network

Legacy database migration & long-term maintenance

Led end-to-end migration of legacy, monolithic applications and databases onto modern cloud platforms — normalising relational schemas, writing and tuning complex SQL queries, and building the APIs that connected everything together. Ran client requirements gathering and led development with a small team, then maintained the resulting systems for twenty years.

Delivered across NHS Wales and the healthcare university network of Cardiff, Bangor, and Wrexham, connecting healthcare providers, students, and universities — then extended beyond Wales to Trinity College Dublin, the University of Galway, the University of Edinburgh, and Edith Cowan University in Perth, Australia.

Valley Diagnostics

High-volume diagnostic data processing on AWS

Thousands of people managing COPD were submitting lateral flow test results from home every day. I built the system behind a mobile app that processed those results at scale, hosted on secure AWS cloud infrastructure so results could be handled reliably and safely no matter how much testing was happening on any given day.

South Staffordshire & Shropshire Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust

Secure mobile dictation for clinical teams

Technical delivery lead for a secure mobile dictation application used by clinical psychologists to update client files by voice while working in the community. Voice recordings were securely transmitted to a central typing pool within the Trust for transcription and addition to confidential patient notes.

Worked directly with the client on site throughout delivery, providing regular in-person updates and ensuring the solution met the Trust's security and confidentiality requirements.

Membership platform strategy & product leadership

SubHub is a platform used by organisations across the world to manage their paying members and subscribers.
