Cardiff University, NHS Wales & the Welsh University Network 2004–2024

Valley Diagnostics 2020–2021

High-volume diagnostic data processing on AWS

Thousands of people managing COPD were submitting lateral flow test results from home every day. I built the system that processed those results reliably and securely at scale, on AWS cloud infrastructure, however much testing was happening on any given day.

South Staffordshire & Shropshire Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust

Secure mobile dictation for clinical teams

As technical delivery lead, I designed and built a secure mobile app that let clinical psychologists dictate client notes by voice while working in the community — recordings were sent securely to a central typing pool for transcription into confidential patient files. I visited the Trust regularly throughout the project to run through builds and gather feedback, making sure the solution was as trustworthy as the paper records it replaced.

SubHub

Membership platform strategy & product leadership

SubHub is a platform used by organisations worldwide to manage paying members and subscribers. I led technical strategy and the development team, refocusing the product and cutting running costs — while keeping member records reliable and well organised for the people who depended on them.
