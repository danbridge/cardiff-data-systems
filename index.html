Does it work without you?

Does your business work without you?

In most firms, a lot of how things really get done lives in a few people’s heads and a few spreadsheets. When they’re away, or leave, it shows.

We work out how your business actually runs and write it down: the steps, the systems and the workarounds. Then we fix what’s costing you time and money, so the business runs on a process, not on people quietly covering the gaps.

  • A plain-English picture of how work really happens.
  • The few fixes worth making first.
  • Everything written down, so it’s yours.

How we work

One person, three disciplines.

Consultants advise but don’t build. Developers build but don’t understand your business. Your IT provider keeps things running. We do the bit in between: working inside your organisation to understand how it runs, find where the time and money go, and build the fix.

  • Understand. Business analysis: how work really happens.
  • Find. Data science: where time and cost are leaking, and what your records can tell you.
  • Build. Engineering, backed by 30 years of software development.

Embedded with you, not parachuted in.
