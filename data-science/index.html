Data science

Is there something in your data?

Most organisations hold years of records that could answer useful questions. Use our “Data Science Ready” checker below to see where data science could genuinely help, and where it’s too early.

What it is

Data science, in plain English.

Your organisation already records what happens: enquiries, clients, invoices, jobs, emails. Data science uses that history to answer three kinds of question.

  • What is actually going on? A clear picture of what your records really show, beyond the monthly report.
  • Why does it keep happening? The patterns behind late payments, overrunning jobs or enquiries that go nowhere.
  • What is likely to happen next? Forecasts and early warnings, so you plan rather than react.

How it can help

Better decisions, fewer guesses.

It isn't magic. It works when you have enough history, records that are reasonably consistent, and a decision worth improving. Often the first win is simply seeing clearly what you already hold.
