Privacy

How we look after your details.

Short version: we don’t store anything about you or your business until we’ve checked with you. We only use what you send us to reply to you, we don’t sell it, and we don’t track you.

The details

Privacy notice

Our promise

We don’t store any data about you or your business until we’ve checked with you. When you send us a message, it arrives in our inbox and nowhere else. We don’t add you to a customer database, a mailing list or any other record, and we don’t keep notes about you or your firm, until we’ve spoken and you’re happy for us to.

Who we are

Cardiff Data Systems, run by Dan Bridge, is the data controller for information collected on this website. Contact: dan@cardiffdatasystems.co.uk.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the “Your jobs” form or by email: the description of your job, your name, your email address, and optionally your firm and phone number. If you email us, we also keep that email.

Why we use it

To read your message and reply to you, and if you ask us to, to talk about doing some work for you. Our legal basis is that you have asked us to get in touch (and, where relevant, our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries).

Who sees it

Dan Bridge. Your message is delivered to us by email, so it passes through our email provider and, where used, the service that carries form messages to us. They process it on our behalf. We don’t sell your details or use them for marketing to other people.

How long we keep it

Until we’ve checked with you, your message stays only in our inbox. If we don’t end up working together, we delete it within 12 months. If we do work together, we keep the records we need for the work and for our accounts.

Cookies and tracking

We don’t use cookies or analytics on this site. Our pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers and your IP address is visible to them. The two checkers (AI agents and data science) run entirely in your browser: nothing you choose in them is sent to us unless you press the button to email us the results.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how we use it. Just email us. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
