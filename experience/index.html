Background

From C on Dec Alphas to cloud and AI.

I started as a software engineer in Cambridge in 1996, helped build Reuters Business Briefing in London, and ran engineering for a VC-funded start-up in San Francisco.

Back in Cardiff I spent twenty years as the technical lead for a suite of practice-placement systems used across NHS Wales and the university healthcare network, taking them from desktop Access databases to cloud platforms and keeping them running reliably as they spread to Ireland, Scotland and Australia. Along the way I was CTO of a games studio and led product teams for agencies and SaaS companies. Today I work with a small number of organisations at a time, as a hands-on technical resource covering business analysis, auditing and engineering.

The approach

The work that matters is often hidden beneath the surface.

I take legacy, poorly documented systems and turn them into something clear, dependable, and trusted by the people who rely on them every day.

Three decades as a hands-on software engineer and CTO, grounded in a Computer Science degree, have taught me that solving the right problem in a way that's useful and reliable matters more than building something that just looks clever — a trap it's easy for developers to fall into. I'm particularly drawn to organisations with a strong mission to help people — the technology matters most when it's in service of something worth doing.

How I work

A person who understands the problem and the code.

I sit down directly with the people who actually run things — not through a layer of business analysts — to properly understand the real problem, then lead design and development with a small, hands-on team. I write things down clearly and make that documentation freely available, so your understanding of your own systems never depends on one person being around — including me.
